**USC Raven** is a flapping-wing UAV built around a *super-compliant* wing: rather than driving a rigid airfoil, each wing is independently actuated and allowed to deform passively under aerodynamic load, trading mechanical complexity for a lighter, more forgiving structure.

Initiated and led with a team of six at the **Fluid-Structure Interactions Lab, USC** (advisor: Prof. Mitul Luhar), the project covered the full arc from lightweight mechatronic design through fabrication to flight test. Along the way we built a **fan-array wind tunnel** &mdash; then an unusual facility for UAV-scale aerodynamic testing &mdash; to characterize the wing before taking it outdoors.

The aircraft achieved **the first successful sustained outdoor flight** of a compliant-wing ornithopter of its kind.
