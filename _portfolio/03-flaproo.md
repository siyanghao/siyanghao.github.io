**Flaproo** is a flapping-wing robot designed as a controllable stand-in for a bird: unlike a live animal, it repeats the same wingbeat on demand, which makes it possible to isolate the aerodynamics of vortex-wake interaction.

The design adds **active wing twisting and folding** to a conventional flapping mechanism, so the wing's shape through the stroke cycle becomes an experimental variable rather than a fixed property of the airframe. Mounted in the wind tunnel alongside the vortex generator used in the *Surfing on Vortices* experiments, it provides direct force and torque measurements to complement the live-bird data.

The robot was featured by *National Geographic*.
